Research programme · Aug 2025 – Oct 2026 · closing report

Binance Edge Search

Fourteen months of pre-registered tests for a tradeable edge on Binance USDT-M futures: BTC direction from 5 seconds to 4 hours, cross-sectional factors across 860 perpetuals, and delta-neutral funding carry.

The signals are real. None of them pays at a retail fee tier: no tested strategy beat cash after costs.

Why

Five causes that kept recurring

Fees versus move size

Required accuracy is (1 + fee / move) / 2. On 90 s trigger bars the move is 10–13 bp, so the target is 85–95 % accuracy. Models reached 52–55 %.

Signal half-life versus latency

The strongest direction information halves every ~30 s. By the time an order lands, most of the predicted move has happened.

The horizon trade-off

Longer horizons give moves large enough to pay fees, but the information is gone: IC ≈ 0 from 15 minutes to an hour.

Payoff shape

"Win small, lose big" appeared three separate times: the 90 s barrier book, the 4 h mean-reversion rule, and short legs on small coins that pump 150–500 % in a day.

Premia, not predictions

The only consistent positive was funding carry, a premium you collect. It shrank as capital arrived and is now about the risk-free rate on the majors.

Open doors

What would change the verdict

  • A far cheaper fee tier or a maker-rebate venue. The 30–90 s ranking signal clears a maker fee at a 30 s hold with perfect sign. The untested run.013 maker fill-timing panel is the experiment aimed at the adverse-selection half.
  • Sub-second, co-located execution. The big-move tail is worth 11–14 bp at zero delay. Capturing it is an infrastructure business, not a modelling one.
  • Cheap spot borrow on negative-funding coins. A live borrow-rate check would settle whether any of the +37 %/yr mirror survives.
  • Options. Selling volatility with the detector as a veto was never tested; no implied-volatility history was collected.
Method

The controls that actually caught errors

  1. Sample length. The 4 h rule passed every structural control on one year and was an artifact over seven.
  2. One position at a time. Non-overlapping P&L caught a 9.4× inflation no other check saw.
  3. A drift benchmark. Three apparent passes were just "always long" on bars where the price rose 87 % of the time.
  4. Entry-delay rows. The only control that separated a real +11 bp tail from a tradeable one.
  5. Permutation nulls and Holm correction across every cell tested.
  6. An economic pre-check before building: required accuracy = max((1 + fee / move) / 2, share of up-moves). Applied early, it would have saved most of runs 007–011.
  7. Tests that run the real code. Leak tests that scramble the future, planted-signal and null worlds. Two verdict bugs were found only by re-deriving results from saved data.
  8. Rank IC is not P&L. Strong IC with losing books appeared twice. Report median and mean, and the worst 1 % of days.
